# Participation Canvas

<div class="toolkit-intro">

<p class="toolkit-purpose"><strong>What this tool is for:</strong> Use this canvas to design how members will participate in your Community of Practice.</p>

<p>People will participate in different ways and at different levels. A healthy community creates multiple pathways for people to move from observing to asking, contributing, collaborating and eventually leading.</p>

</div>

<div class="toolkit-meta">

<div class="toolkit-meta-box">
<strong>Use this when</strong>
You are designing participation, onboarding or community activities.
</div>

<div class="toolkit-meta-box">
<strong>Work on it</strong>
With your founding group and, where possible, potential members.
</div>

</div>

## How to use this tool

1. Think about the different ways people might participate.
2. Make sure participation does not depend only on attending formal events.
3. Consider how someone can move towards deeper involvement.
4. Test your ideas with members and revise them as the community develops.
