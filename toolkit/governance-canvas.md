# Governance Canvas

<div class="toolkit-intro">

<p class="toolkit-purpose"><strong>What this tool is for:</strong> Use this canvas to design a governance model that provides enough structure without unnecessarily centralising control.</p>

<p>The aim is to make responsibilities and decision-making clear while creating opportunities for members to take ownership.</p>

</div>

<div class="toolkit-meta">

<div class="toolkit-meta-box">
<strong>Use this when</strong>
You are establishing or reviewing leadership and decision-making arrangements.
</div>

<div class="toolkit-meta-box">
<strong>Work on it</strong>
With the founding group and, where appropriate, community members.
</div>

</div>

## How to use this tool

1. Identify what needs coordination.
2. Decide which decisions need collective input.
3. Identify opportunities for members to lead.
4. Consider where influence currently sits.
5. Build in continuity so the community does not depend on one person.

## Remember

Good governance should provide enough structure to support the community without unnecessarily centralising control.

The aim is not to create bureaucracy. It is to create the conditions for members to participate, contribute and take ownership.
