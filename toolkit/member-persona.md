# Member Profile

<div class="toolkit-intro">

<p class="toolkit-purpose"><strong>What this tool is for:</strong> Use this template to understand one type of member in your Community of Practice.</p>

<p>Create a separate profile for each important member group. The aim is not to describe every individual member, but to develop useful hypotheses about their needs, motivations, barriers and potential contributions.</p>

</div>

<div class="toolkit-meta">

<div class="toolkit-meta-box">
<strong>Use this when</strong>
You are defining who your community is for or reviewing whether it is meeting members' needs.
</div>

<div class="toolkit-meta-box">
<strong>Work on it</strong>
Individually, with your founding group, or with potential members.
</div>

</div>

## How to use this tool

1. Choose one important member group.
2. Complete the profile based on what you currently know.
3. Identify assumptions you need to test.
4. Speak to real people from this group and update the profile as you learn more.

## Your output

By completing this tool, you should have a working hypothesis about:

- who this member group is;
- why they might participate;
- what they need;
- what they can contribute;
- what might prevent participation;
- what would make participation valuable;
- how they could become more deeply involved over time.

Remember that the profile is a hypothesis. Validate it with real people and update it as you learn more.
