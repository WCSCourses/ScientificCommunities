# Communication & Infrastructure Canvas

<div class="toolkit-intro">

<p class="toolkit-purpose"><strong>What this tool is for:</strong> Use this canvas to decide what technology and communication infrastructure your community actually needs.</p>

<p>Start with community behaviours rather than platforms. Choose technology because it supports something members need to do — not simply because the technology is available.</p>

</div>

<div class="toolkit-meta">

<div class="toolkit-meta-box">
<strong>Use this when</strong>
You are choosing communication channels, platforms or shared infrastructure.
</div>

<div class="toolkit-meta-box">
<strong>Work on it</strong>
With your founding group and potential members.
</div>

</div>

## How to use this tool

1. Identify what members need to do.
2. Choose the simplest infrastructure that supports those behaviours.
3. Define what each channel is for.
4. Consider accessibility, ownership and maintenance.
5. Avoid adding platforms unless there is a clear community need.

## Your output

By completing this canvas, you should have:

- a defined purpose for each additional channel;
- communication norms;
- a plan for managing shared knowledge;
- identified accessibility considerations;
- clear infrastructure ownership;
- a minimum viable technology setup.
