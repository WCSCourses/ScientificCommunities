# Community Evaluation Canvas

Use this canvas to evaluate whether your Community of Practice is creating meaningful value.

The aim is not to measure everything.

Instead, use a small number of meaningful indicators to understand participation, engagement, relationships, learning, changes in practice and wider outcomes.

# 13. Decide what happens next

Use your evidence to decide whether the community should:

### Continue

The community is creating value and should continue broadly as it is.

### Adapt

The community is valuable but something needs to change.

### Refocus

The original purpose is no longer sufficient.

### Pause

There is currently insufficient value or capacity to continue.

### Close

The community has fulfilled its purpose or is no longer the appropriate mechanism.

## Sources & foundations

### Wenger-Trayner & Wenger-Trayner

Wenger-Trayner, E. & Wenger-Trayner, B. (2015). *Introduction to Communities of Practice: A Brief Overview of the Concept and its Uses.*

This source informs the emphasis on assessing CoP value through a combination of quantitative and qualitative evidence, while recognising that attribution of outcomes may be complex.

It also provides examples of community activities and interactions that can be used to identify meaningful engagement.

### The Less Visible Leader

Robinson, A. (2012). *The Less Visible Leader: Emerging Leadership Models for Environmental Networks, Coalitions, and Collaboratives*. Institute for Conservation Leadership.

This source informs the emphasis on assessing relationships and network strength rather than relying only on participation counts.

### Our synthesis

The Community Evaluation Canvas, including the evidence chain, indicator-selection table and Community Health Check, was developed specifically for this guide.

It is an original practical synthesis rather than a reproduction of any of the sources above.
