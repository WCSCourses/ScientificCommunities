# 90-Day Community Launch Plan

<div class="toolkit-intro">

<p class="toolkit-purpose"><strong>What this tool is for:</strong> Use this planner to move from preparation to an active Community of Practice.</p>

<p>The first three months are an opportunity to test your assumptions, learn what members value and establish patterns of participation. The plan should therefore remain flexible.</p>

</div>

<div class="toolkit-meta">

<div class="toolkit-meta-box">
<strong>Use this when</strong>
You are preparing to launch a new Community of Practice.
</div>

<div class="toolkit-meta-box">
<strong>Work on it</strong>
With your founding group and potential members.
</div>

</div>

## How to use this tool

1. Complete the preparation sections before launch.
2. Design the first interaction around a genuine shared challenge.
3. Use the first three months to move from connection towards contribution and co-creation.
4. Review what you are learning rather than simply following the plan rigidly.

# Evidence of early community health

Look for signs that the community is beginning to function through member interaction rather than simply organiser-led activity.

- [ ] Members return
- [ ] Members ask questions
- [ ] Members answer one another
- [ ] Members share resources
- [ ] Members introduce others
- [ ] Members organise activities
- [ ] Members collaborate
- [ ] Members take leadership
- [ ] New relationships are developing
- [ ] Shared practice is becoming visible

## Your output

By completing this plan, you should have:

- a clear plan for your first interaction;
- follow-up actions for the first month;
- a progression from connection to contribution and co-creation;
- defined opportunities for member contribution;
- a 90-day review point;
- early indicators of community health.

At the end of the 90 days, use the **Community Evaluation Canvas** to look more systematically at participation, relationships, learning, change in practice and wider outcomes.

## Remember

The plan is a starting point, not a contract.

Pay attention to what members actually value and adapt the community as you learn.
