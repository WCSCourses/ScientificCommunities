# Community Ownership Canvas

<div class="toolkit-intro">

<p class="toolkit-purpose"><strong>What this tool is for:</strong> Use this canvas to assess and strengthen member ownership, distributed responsibility and succession within your Community of Practice.</p>

<p>The aim is to move gradually from organiser-led activity towards shared responsibility, while making sure members have the support and opportunities they need to contribute.</p>

</div>

<div class="toolkit-meta">

<div class="toolkit-meta-box">
<strong>Use this when</strong>
Your community is established and you want to strengthen member ownership and distributed leadership.
</div>

<div class="toolkit-meta-box">
<strong>Work on it</strong>
With organisers, facilitators and community members.
</div>

</div>

## How to use this tool

1. Map where responsibility and influence currently sit.
2. Identify opportunities for members to contribute.
3. Choose one responsibility that could be transferred.
4. Consider leadership development and succession.
5. Review whether leadership opportunities are genuinely accessible.

## Sources & foundations

This canvas is an original synthesis developed for this guide. It draws on established approaches to Communities of Practice, collaborative leadership and community development.

### Wenger-Trayner & Wenger-Trayner

Wenger-Trayner, E. & Wenger-Trayner, B. (2024). *Communities of Practice in and across Organizations: A Guidebook.*

This source informs the emphasis on participation, community cultivation, shared learning and the development of community leadership.

[Explore the guidebook →](https://www.wenger-trayner.com/cop-guidebook/)

### The Less Visible Leader

Robinson, A. (2012). *The Less Visible Leader: Emerging Leadership Models for Environmental Networks, Coalitions, and Collaboratives*. Institute for Conservation Leadership.

This source informs the emphasis on distributed leadership, shared responsibility, creating opportunities for others to lead and reducing dependence on a single leader.

### Community Tool Box

Center for Community Health and Development, University of Kansas. *Community Tool Box.*

The Community Tool Box provides practical guidance on community leadership, participation, action planning and sustaining community initiatives.

[Explore the Community Tool Box →](https://ctb.ku.edu/)

### About this canvas

The questions, tables and leadership pathway in this canvas were developed specifically for this guide. They are not reproduced from the sources above.
